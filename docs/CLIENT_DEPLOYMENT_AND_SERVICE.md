# Your Private Leads Desk Deployment

Leads Desk is delivered as a private lead-management system for your organisation. Your team receives a ready-to-use workspace, while Run Rentless handles the technical setup, security, backups, testing, and launch.

## What You Receive

Your package includes:

- a private Leads Desk installation
- a private PostgreSQL database
- a dedicated business domain
- company branding and workspace configuration
- administrator and team access setup
- lead stages, sources, custom fields, reports, and forms
- agreed lead-capture integrations
- migration of the agreed existing lead data
- team training and launch support
- automatic backup setup
- 24 months of infrastructure hosting
- a perpetual licence to use the installed version

Your Leads Desk installation and business records are not shared with other Leads Desk customers.

## What We Need From You

Before setup begins, provide:

- your company name and logo
- your preferred domain name
- the primary administrator's name and email
- your team member list
- your lead stages and sales process
- any existing lead or customer data to import
- the integrations included in your package
- the names of people attending training

You do not need to configure a server, database, source-code repository, security tool, or backup process.

## How Launch Works

1. **Business setup:** You provide your branding, users, sales process, data, and agreed integration requirements.
2. **Private deployment:** Run Rentless prepares your hosting, database, domain, and Leads Desk installation.
3. **Workspace configuration:** We configure branding, permissions, lead stages, fields, reports, forms, and agreed integrations.
4. **Data migration:** We import and validate the agreed existing lead or customer data.
5. **Release testing:** We test login, permissions, lead workflows, forms, backups, security, and important reports.
6. **Training and handover:** Your administrator and team receive access, training, renewal information, and support details.

## Hosting And Capacity

The standard package starts with infrastructure suitable for the agreed initial usage profile, typically:

- up to approximately 100 staff accounts
- up to approximately 100,000 lead records
- normal sales-team activity
- moderate reports and regular lead intake

This capacity is approved only after the final client deployment passes its measured load, backup, restore, and workflow checks. If usage, data volume, integrations, or reporting demands grow significantly, Run Rentless may recommend a larger hosting plan. Any future infrastructure upgrade is agreed with you before it is applied.

The initial package includes 24 months of infrastructure hosting. You are responsible for renewing the hosting after that included period. Run Rentless will provide renewal information before the due date.

## Backups And Recovery

Backups run automatically. You do not need to download, upload, rotate, or delete backup files.

The production setup is designed to protect against accidental deletion, database errors, failed updates, corruption, and server failure. Run Rentless verifies the backup process before handover and performs an isolated restore test as part of production acceptance.

Restore assistance and ongoing backup monitoring can be included in a maintenance agreement.

## Updates And Support

Run Rentless manages approved Leads Desk releases. Before an update, we back up the production database, apply the approved release, run its database migration once, and verify the important workflows.

The implementation package includes 30 days of post-launch support for defects and deployment-related issues.

Optional annual maintenance can include:

- security updates and bug fixes
- compatibility updates
- backup monitoring and restore assistance
- infrastructure checks
- priority support
- approved product improvements

You can continue using the installed version without an annual maintenance agreement, provided the hosting remains active and technically compatible. Future features, updates, monitoring, and priority support may require an active agreement.

## Ownership

You control:

- your business data
- your user accounts
- your private database
- your domain
- your hosting account
- your installed Leads Desk version

Run Rentless retains ownership of the Leads Desk source code, reusable product architecture, shared components, future versions, and the Leads Desk brand. Your organisation receives a perpetual licence to use its installed version; it is not purchasing exclusive ownership of the Leads Desk product.
