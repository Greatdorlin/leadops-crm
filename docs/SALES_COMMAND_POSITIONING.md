# Leads Desk Sales Command Positioning

## Product Position

Leads Desk is a private sales command center.

It is not just:

- a contact database
- a generic CRM clone
- a report folder

It helps businesses stop losing leads they already paid for.

## Headline

Stop losing leads your business already paid for.

## Subheadline

Capture leads from your website, forms, ads, and team channels, assign follow-up, track performance, and see exactly what is moving, stuck, or overdue.

## Core Value Promise

Every lead captured. Every follow-up assigned. Every salesperson visible. Every source measured.

## Business Pain Solved

- leads are scattered across forms, calls, WhatsApp, ads, referrals, walk-ins, and spreadsheets
- managers cannot tell who is following up
- follow-ups are forgotten
- leads go cold
- teams miss sales targets
- source performance is unclear
- reporting is manual or weak

## Ownership Promise

- privately installed
- your data belongs to you
- no per-user CRM subscription

## Product Lens For Future Work

Every future feature should strengthen one or more of these:

- capture
- assignment
- follow-up discipline
- sales visibility
- target progress
- reporting clarity
- client control
